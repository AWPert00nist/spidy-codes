About Treebo

Treebo is a budget hotel chain that runs a tech-enabled franchise model: it takes on hotels exclusively and supplies branding, customer acquisition and management software (CB Insights; Inc42). Its consolidated revenue from operations was about ₹109 crore in FY24, up around 22% (Entrackr), with accommodation about 95% of it.

Competitors

  • Entrackr names FabHotels and Bloom Hotels as direct competitors (Entrackr).
  • Travellers also compare on travel booking platforms, which list the same rooms.

Value

A consistent, clean and affordable stay, with a promise a traveller can trust before arriving. Property counts differ widely between sources, so I do not quote one.

Revenue figures are from press coverage of company filings. Check the filings before reuse.

Personas

Illustrative archetypes I made up, not real people. Hypotheses are labelled. Focus is the search-led user; browsing-led exploration is out of scope.

The solo business traveller

Who: Travels for work, books a night or two near the office

Goals: A clean, predictable room and an easy invoice

Pain points: Hypothesis: re-enters dates and city on each trip

Opportunity: Recent searches and saved preferences

The weekend family

Who: Parents with children on a short leisure trip

Goals: Know what is included before paying

Pain points: Fees surprise users late; the convenience fee is non-refundable (Treebo FAQ)

Opportunity: Show all fees at room selection

The last-minute booker

Who: Books the same day after a plan change

Goals: Instant confirmation and a flexible cancel rule

Pain points: A booking shown as cancelled after payment is reported (Trustpilot)

Opportunity: Clear payment status and a refund tracker

Key UX Elements

Illustrative wireframes of proposed screens. They are not screenshots of Treebo's app, and all copy and amounts are placeholders.

1. Search: city, dates and guests, with recent searches

2. Results: total price with taxes, free-cancel filter

3. Hotel page: inclusions and cancellation rule above the fold

4. Checkout: every charge, plus the refund promise

Improvements

My suggestions, not claims about Treebo's roadmap.

1. Total price in results

Show the final price with taxes and fees on each card. Why: Fees revealed late erode trust and cause drop-off at checkout. Risk: Higher headline price versus rivals who hide fees.

2. Cancellation rule up front

Put what is included and the cancellation rule above the fold on the hotel page. Why: Flexibility is a main reason to book directly. Risk: Strict rules shown early may lower conversion.

3. Show fees at room selection

Move the convenience fee from the last step to room selection, and say whether it is refundable. Why: The FAQ says it is non-refundable, which surprises people at cancellation. Risk: Revenue impact if the fee is discouraged.

4. Payment status and refund tracker

If payment succeeds but confirmation is delayed, show a clear status. After cancellation, show the refund stage and date. Why: Reviews report cancelled bookings after payment and slow refunds. Risk: Needs reliable payment-gateway status data.

Key Success Metrics

North Star

Stays completed per month (booked and checked in, not cancelled).

L1: Conversion

Checkout starts to paid bookings.

  • Also payment success rate.

L2: Retention

Second booking within 90 days / first bookings.

  • Plus direct-app share of bookings.

Guardrails

  • Days from cancellation to refund, and refund-related support contacts per 1,000 bookings.
  • Cancellation rate and no-show rate.
  • Average rating after stay and NPS.
