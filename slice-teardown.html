What's in it for slice?

slice began as a card and UPI app and merged with North East Small Finance Bank, effective 27 October 2024 (YourStory; Entrackr). Since then, a new user has to become a bank customer, and that means identity verification before the app can show its value.

  • Drop-off is the main risk. Digital account opening loses a large share of users at KYC; one UX study puts abandonment at up to 60% (Markswebb). Every verified account counts.
  • Trust is built early or not at all. Users are asked for PAN, selfie and video before they have seen the product.

Public information on slice's current flow is thin. Where I describe a step I could not confirm, I label it a hypothesis. Forum posts are individual reports, not measured rates.

Personas

Illustrative archetypes I made up, not real people. Hypotheses are labelled.

The first-jobber

Who: 23, just started a first salaried job in Bengaluru

Goals: A free, simple savings account with good interest

Pain points: Hypothesis: unsure what documents are needed and drops off to find them

Opportunity: A "keep these ready" checklist with time estimate

The existing slice user

Who: Already uses the slice card or UPI app

Goals: Move to the bank account without starting from scratch

Pain points: Post-merger, existing users move to the bank (YourStory); the migration steps are not clearly public

Opportunity: Pre-fill known details and explain what changes

The freelancer

Who: Irregular income, name spelled differently across documents

Goals: Open an account without a branch visit

Pain points: Name errors in CKYC data led to a rejected account in one reported case (DesiDime)

Opportunity: Show fetched name, allow corrections in place

Good & Bad

What works (reported)

  • Fetching data from CKYC and using video KYC means no document upload for many users (DesiDime).
  • Fully digital account opening saves a branch visit, which is the stated promise of the bank.

What hurts (reported or likely)

  • CKYC name errors can cause a rejection with no easy fix (DesiDime).
  • Accounts opened via CKYC showing "not serviceable" in the app (DesiDime).
  • Hypothesis: the checklist of documents is not shown up front.

Suggested Improvements

My suggestions, not claims about slice's roadmap.

1. Tell users what to keep ready

Open with a checklist and a time estimate before any field. Why: Users who leave to find a document often do not return. Risk: A long checklist can scare people off, so keep it to three items.

2. Easy questions first

Ask name and email before PAN and the selfie, with a progress bar and a reason for each ask. Why: Small wins build investment before the sensitive steps. Risk: Longer flow if the extra steps add no information.

3. Show fetched data and allow fixes

After the CKYC fetch, show the name and let the user correct or switch method without restarting. Why: Mismatch rejections are a reported cause of failure. Risk: Needs a safe correction path to satisfy compliance.

4. Save and resume

Keep progress after KYC steps and send a reminder with a deep link to the exact step. Why: Users who drop off mid-way are the cheapest to win back. Risk: Reminders must respect consent and frequency limits.

Key Metrics

North Star

Verified accounts opened per day.

  • Counts only completed, activated accounts.

L1: Funnel completion

% of installs that finish video KYC.

  • Break down by step to find the biggest drop.

L2: Recovery

% of users who leave mid-flow and later finish.

  • Measures save-and-resume and reminders.

Guardrails

  • KYC rejection and manual-review rates, so easier flows do not weaken compliance.
  • Fraud and mismatch rates by method.
  • Time to complete and support contacts per 1,000 onboardings.
